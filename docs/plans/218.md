# #218: the co-sign QR exchange scans both codes and appends the co-signature

## Objective

The QR co-sign flow in `CoSignActivity` works end to end:

1. The owner picks a signed JPEG with `OpenDocument` (persistable read and
   write) and shows the request QR `{"v":1,"hash","alg","from"}`. Media with no
   signature data is refused with a message.
2. The witness scans the request QR, sees the content hash and the owner's
   fingerprint, approves, signs the hash and shows the response QR
   `{"v":1,"sig","fp","name","alg"}`.
3. The owner scans the response QR; it becomes an `ATTESTOR` `SignatureEntry`
   appended to the media. A key that has already signed is refused and the
   media is unchanged.

   - Camera permission through one choke point: `QrScannerActivity` asks for
     CAMERA itself (`CameraPermissionGate`) and finishes with
     `RESULT_CAMERA_DENIED`; every launcher reads its result through
     `QrScanResult.from` and shows `qr_camera_denied`. `OathActivity` drops its
     own pre-check. Tests: `ui/QrScannerPermissionGateTest` (gate steps and
     result mapping) and `ui/QrScannerChokePointTest` (no launcher checks
     CAMERA itself or skips `QrScanResult.from`; only the allow-listed files
     build a `QrCodeScannerHelper`).
   - The pending exchange survives recreation: selected Uri, witness mode and
     signing key id go to `onSaveInstanceState`; `onCreate` restores them
     before results are delivered; the request dialog waits for the key list;
     the response append needs only the Uri and waits for the media reload.
   - Instrumented tests drive the QR flow: picker stub on
     `ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT`, scanner stubbed by component; approval, append
     and duplicate refusal assert the saved media; denial feedback on both
     scan buttons; recreation while the real scanner is open for each scan.

## Out of scope

- BLE mode (`cosign.request` over a live session, held #177).
- Verifying the witness signature on the owner device: the response carries
  only the key fingerprint; verification stays with the verify flow.
- Non-JPEG media: `readSignatureFromUri` reads EXIF only.
- `ContactManagementActivity`'s inline scanner keeps its own CAMERA request
  (allow-listed in the choke-point test).
