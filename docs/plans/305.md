# 305: one list screen for the BLE account choice

## Objective

When a BLE GetAssertion has no allowList and the sharing policy shows more
than one discoverable passkey for the RP, the owner picks the account on the
phone. Today `BiometricFidoUserPrompt.chooseAccount` shows one biometric
prompt per account in turn. Replace that with one list screen: the accounts
in the order given, the RP as subtitle, and a Cancel button.

## Slices, in order

1. **Plan** (this file).
2. **Tests** (fail on the base):
   - `BiometricFidoUserPromptTest`: `chooseAccount` through a fake
     launcher's `requestAccountChoice`: index 2 of 3 is picked, a decline
     gives null, an index outside the list gives null, no launcher gives
     null, a `CancellationException` is rethrown.
   - `TransparentBiometricActivityAccountChoiceTest` (Robolectric, real
     launcher and activity): the list shows every account in order as plain
     text, a tap answers its index, Cancel and back answer null, and a
     cancelled caller finishes the activity.
3. **Implementation**:
   - `TransparentBiometricActivity`: an account-choice mode, chosen by the
     accounts extra. A dialog lists the accounts as plain text with the RP
     as subtitle and a Cancel button; it honours `showOnLockScreen` and
     claims the screen as the prompt mode does. A tap answers success with
     the chosen index; Cancel, back or dismissal answer user-cancelled. No
     new manifest entry.
   - `BiometricActivityLauncher.requestAccountChoice(title, subtitle,
     accounts, showOnLockScreen, onLaunch): Int?` on the same pending
     request queue and result broadcast as `requestAuthentication`. A
     cancel answers null, as does an index outside `0 until accounts.size`.
     Coroutine cancellation finishes the activity (the queue's withdraw).
   - `BiometricFidoUserPrompt.chooseAccount`: one `requestAccountChoice`
     inside `onLockScreen("getAssertion", rpId)`, keeping its error
     mapping.
4. **Docs**: `docs/fido2-phone-backend/architecture.md` describes the list
   screen, and its "Left:" line keeps only device verification.

## Out of scope

- `FidoUserPrompt`, `Ctap2Authenticator`, `AndroidFidoCredentialBackend`:
  unchanged. The verify prompt after the choice stays as it is.
- `docs/SPEC.md` and `ROADMAP.md`.
- Verification on a device against a CTAP2 BLE client.
