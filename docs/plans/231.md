# #231: audit desktop backup store and restore

## Objective

A paired desktop can store (`local.createBackup`) and fetch
(`local.restoreBackup`) opaque backups on the phone, and neither appears in
the audit log. Add two audit operation types and record one entry per
successful call, naming the requesting device the way `local.sign` does
(`connectedDevicePublicKeyHex` and `pairedDeviceRepository`).

## Slices, in order

1. Plan (this file).
2. Failing tests:
   - `AuditLoggerDispatchTest`: `logBackupCreated` records BACKUP_CREATED with
     device id/name and details `{backupId, sizeBytes}`; `logBackupRestored`
     records BACKUP_RESTORED with details `{backupId, sizeBytes}`.
   - New `protocol/BackupHandlerAuditTest`: a valid `local.createBackup`
     records one BACKUP_CREATED entry holding the returned `backup_id` and the
     byte count; `local.restoreBackup` (latest and by id) records one
     BACKUP_RESTORED entry with that id; missing or invalid `backup_data` and
     an unknown id record no success entry; no entry's details hold the
     backup bytes or their base64.
3. Fix: `AuditOperationType.BACKUP_CREATED` and `BACKUP_RESTORED` appended
   at the end of the enum (stored by name, so no Room migration);
   `AuditLogger.logBackupCreated` / `logBackupRestored`; audit screen labels
   and strings; calls from `handleCreateBackup` and `handleRestoreBackup`
   after success.
4. `docs/desktop-backup-store/architecture.md` states what is audited.

## Out of scope

- `backup/BackupStore.kt` (an open change rewrites it).
- `BackupHandlerWireTest` (an open change edits it).
- Auditing failed backup calls; the `label`, which is desktop-supplied text.
- Backups made through `XKeyGattService` directly.
