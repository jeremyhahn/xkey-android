# Plan #259: SPEC row co-signing

## Objective

Row `co-signing` of docs/SPEC.md: multi-party signing of media by QR or BLE
exchange (`cosign.request`, `cosign.response`), Android-only. The row is
already marked `shipped` (QR exchange landed in #246); what remains are the
row's folded items: #177 (cosign.request signs only after the owner approves
it on the phone) and its open PR #179.

## Slices

1. Fold PR #179: merge its head `row/177-cosign-approval` into
   `row/259-co-signing` with a merge commit (no rebase). Its plan is
   docs/plans/177.md; its tests come with it: `CosignRequestHandlerTest`,
   `KeystoreAliasGateTest`, `PromptEntryPointsTest`,
   `CoSignInitiatorFingerprintTest` and the updated handler, audit and
   approval-flow tests.
2. Review fixes: the anonymous co-sign instrumented test serves its request
   with no caller; an initiator QR names a desktop-created FIDO2 key by its
   fingerprint. `KeystoreManager.keyRowAlias` is the one key-row-to-alias
   mapping, and `KeystoreAliasNamingChokePointTest` fails on a new
   hand-built alias. Signing paths stay on the general key.
3. Merge main and cover the digest gate for every accepted `hashAlgorithm`
   (`CosignRequestHandlerTest`: valid SHA-256/384/512 digests sign only
   after approval; other lengths are refused with no prompt). Co-signing
   signs nothing when another key holds the approved alias.
4. Credential deletion leaves the row. Earlier rounds changed
   `local.deleteKey` and the legacy `deleteKey` (internal-alias refusals,
   Credential Provider metadata removal, a per-credential delete lock and a
   sign-counter hold). None of it is needed by `cosign.request`: an
   approved co-sign is bound to the key material the owner approved, so a
   delete or replacement of that key makes it sign nothing. The legacy
   `deleteKey` matches main. `local.deleteKey` differs from main in two
   hunks, both from this row. (a) It deletes under the key's lock
   (`keyIds.withKeyLock(keyLockId(canonicalId))`), the lock #177's
   `useApprovedKey` holds from the post-approval recheck through the
   signature: without it a delete lands between the recheck and the
   signature, and a co-sign answers for a key already deleted.
   `PromptEntryPointsTest` pins it. (b) Its two spellings of the general key alias
   (`"xkey_key_$id"`) go through `KeystoreManager.generalKeyAlias`, which
   returns the same string, because slice 2's
   `KeystoreAliasNamingChokePointTest` refuses a hand-built alias outside
   `KeystoreManager`. The delete-only tests (`CredentialKeyDeleteTest`,
   `LocalDeleteKeyInternalAliasTest`, `ReservedKeyIdTest`) leave with them.
5. `PasskeySignCounter` keeps no lock for a credential nobody is using: the
   per-credential lock registry is reference-counted and an entry is
   removed when its last holder or waiter leaves (`PasskeySignCounterTest`
   churns many credential IDs, with waiters and failed writes, and checks
   the registry is empty after).
6. Row PR from this branch supersedes #179; docs/SPEC.md keeps the row
   `shipped`.

## Out of scope

- BLE transport changes beyond what #179 carries.
- The desktop contract (the row is Android-only), including credential
  deletion: the delete hardening of earlier rounds (commits fba01996,
  9d8ca66a, b3abfa92, 679649ec, fded3461) is for a key-management or
  passkey row. `KeystoreManager.deleteCredentialKey` and its test stay on
  this branch unused until that row takes them or removes them.
- The desktop OATH expiry policy (commits d0cf77af, f8a6bdd4, 98684afc),
  for a later OATH row.
