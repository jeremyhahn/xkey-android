# Plan: #252 GattServerCccdOrderTest intermittent timeout

## Objective

`GattServerCccdOrderTest > theLatestCccdWriteDecidesTheAcknowledgement`
failed once on main adfc48f with an `AssertionError` from the test's
`eventually` helper: one of its bounded waits timed out. The test pins the
SPEC `ble-transport` rule that the identity acknowledgement goes out the
first moment the latest CCCD write of the frame's connection is an enable.
It must be deterministic.

## Reproduction

- 200 runs of the class (60 rows each), one Gradle run each with `--rerun`,
  no load: 200 passed, 0 failed.
- With CPU load (96 busy loops on 64 cores): 15 runs, all 15 failed, 126
  failed rows. Every failure was a wall-clock bound: mostly
  `timed out waiting until <row>: the acknowledgement is sent on event N`
  on `*I` rows (`E *I`, `E E *I`, `D E *I`, `E *I E` ...) and
  `<row>: the disconnect is handled` (`I X E`, `E X C I`), both 300 ms
  (`PROMPTLY_MS`). No `*E` row failed. Under that load the 5 s hang guards
  also tripped (`I: event 1 is handled`, scope still running after destroy).
- A first attempt repeating the rows 200 times in one JVM ran out of memory
  after 75 clean passes; its failures are that artifact, not the flake.

## Cause

(c): the test decided "promptly" by the wall clock. A `*I` row's 300 ms
covers the whole frame handling, and the disconnect's 300 ms the consumer
waking; a loaded runner misses both. The server's ordering is sound (no
`*E` row failed, and the latest-write rule held in every run). The same
wall clock also bounds the 600 ms readiness wait itself: a runner stalled
between the frame and a releasing enable would drop the acknowledgement.

## Choke point

`TrackingDispatcher` (ScopeDrain.kt) implements `Delay` and holds the
scope's timers: `delay` and `withTimeout` fire only when the test calls
`advanceTimeBy`. Before that, whatever happened was caused by the events
delivered, so "promptly" is causal; wall-clock bounds only stop hung tests.
Rows that expect the wait to expire advance the clock by the timeout.

## Slices

1. Plan (this file).
2. Choke point, `ScopeDrainTest` (bypass: held timers, awaitIdle covers a
   timer-resumed step, and a source scan that no `trackScope` test sleeps,
   reads the wall clock or keeps a `PROMPTLY_MS`), and the sweep:
   CccdOrder, NotificationReady, QueueLifecycle drop their wall-clock
   bounds; EventOrder and Draining need no change (no timer, no clock).
3. 200 runs of the class after the fix, and the loaded run again.

## Out of scope

- The rows of `GattServerCccdOrderTest` and what they assert.
- GattServer.kt, RequestHandler, Noise and pairing code.
- No `Thread.sleep`, `delay` or raised timeouts as the fix.
