# 307: scan finding CVE-2026-102554, guava in Gradle and KGP

Refs #307.

## Cause

The scans report CVE-2026-102554 (HIGH, fixed in guava 33.7.2) in two
build-only copies: guava 33.4.6-jre in Gradle 9.8.1's own `lib/` and guava
33.3.1-jre shaded (as `org.jetbrains.kotlin.com.google.common`) in the
kotlin-gradle-plugin 2.4.21 jar. `.trivyignore` held it until 2026-11-26.
Every guava Gradle resolves, the APK's included, is pinned to 33.7.2
(catalog `guavaJre`, `guavaAndroid`).

## Release check (2026-11-21)

- Gradle: services.gradle.org/versions/current names 9.9.2 (no rc or
  milestone). `ls gradle-9.9.2/lib` shows `guava-33.7.2-jre.jar`;
  9.8.1 shows `guava-33.4.6-jre.jar`.
- KGP: Maven Central `maven-metadata.xml` lists 2.4.21 as the newest
  stable (after it only 2.5.0-Beta1, -Beta2, -RC). `unzip -p` of
  `META-INF/maven/com.google.guava/guava/pom.properties` in its
  kotlin-gradle-plugin jar gives `version=33.3.1-jre`.

## Decision

Renew. Gradle 9.9.2 ships a fixed guava, but no stable KGP does, so a
Gradle bump alone leaves the finding in place; per the removal condition
neither is bumped for it. The ignore is renewed with a dated reason until
2026-12-21. Remove it with the first stable Gradle and KGP that both ship
guava 33.7.2 or later, or by the expiry.

## Result

- `./gradlew :app:dependencies` on `releaseRuntimeClasspath` and
  `debugRuntimeClasspath`: guava only as 33.7.2-android, no 33.4.x or
  33.3.x:
  `com.google.guava:guava:33.3.1-android -> 33.7.2-android`,
  `com.google.guava:guava:32.0.1-android -> 33.7.2-android (*)`,
  `com.google.guava:guava:33.7.2-android (c)` (plus `listenablefuture`
  resolved to `9999.0-empty-to-avoid-conflict-with-guava`).
- trivy 0.74.0 `rootfs --ignore-unfixed --severity CRITICAL,HIGH`: Gradle
  9.8.1 `lib/` and the KGP 2.4.21 jar each report CVE-2026-102554 without
  the ignore file and nothing with it; Gradle 9.9.2 `lib/` reports nothing.
- `make security`: passes. The full image scan runs in CI.

## Out of scope

Any other scan finding, and the Gradle 9.9.2 bump on its own.
