# 308: image scan, lint's kotlin-compiler 32.5.1

Refs #307.

## Cause

The image scan reports five HIGH findings in
`com.android.tools.external.com-intellij:kotlin-compiler:32.5.1`, the jar
lint 32.5.1 (AGP 9.5.1) runs on: shaded protobuf-java 2.6.1
(CVE-2021-22569, CVE-2024-7254), jline-builtins 3.24.1 (CVE-2026-77422) and
jline-remote-telnet 3.24.1 (CVE-2026-56740, CVE-2026-56741). The jar reaches
the image through the warm stage's Gradle cache. `.trivyignore` held all
five until 2026-11-21, expecting the AGP 9.5 bump to drop them; it did not.

## Release check (2026-11-21)

Newest stable AGP on Google's Maven is 9.5.2 (next: 9.6.0-alpha05). Its
kotlin-compiler 32.5.2 is byte-identical to 32.5.1 (same sha256), still with
protobuf-java 2.6.1 and jline 3.24.1. A bump fixes nothing; AGP stays 9.5.1.

## Choke point

One warm-stage RUN step after the lz4-java swap in
`build/docker/devcontainer/Dockerfile`, over every `kotlin-compiler-*.jar`
in `/opt/gradle-ro-cache`; the offline proof that follows runs lintDebug on
the patched cache.

- jline: `org/jline/builtins/` (builtins, remote-telnet, remote-ssh),
  `org/jline/console/` and `org/jline/widget/` and their Maven metadata are
  removed. jdeps over the jar: outside those three packages nothing refers
  to them; the only user of jline outside jline is the Kotlin REPL's line
  reader, which needs only `org/jline/reader` and `org/jline/terminal`
  (kept). lint, lint-api, lint-checks, lint-model and intellij-core 32.5.1
  have no jline reference. After the strip jdeps finds no dangling
  reference. Clears CVE-2026-77422, CVE-2026-56740, CVE-2026-56741.
- protobuf 2.6.1 (relocated to `org/jetbrains/kotlin/protobuf/`) is the
  compiler's metadata reader, used by 40 compiler packages (metadata
  serialization, decompiler): kept, and its two CVEs are ignored with a
  dated reason and a 30-day expiry.

No new pins: the strip downloads nothing. No test commit (image-only).

## Out of scope

The guava ignore (CVE-2026-102554) and any other image finding.
