# Plan: an issued certificate never outlives its issuer (#181)

## Objective

`AndroidCA.issueForPublicKey` sets `notAfter = now + profile.validityDays`
without looking at the signing certificate, and
`generateIntermediateCertificate` sets the intermediate to now + 5 years
without looking at the root. A certificate can therefore outlive the CA that
signed it, and an expired (or not yet valid) CA still issues.

One pure function, `ca/CertificateValidity.kt`, decides the validity window:

- `validityDays <= 0` is refused (`IllegalArgumentException`);
- an issuer that is expired or not yet valid at `now` is refused
  (`IllegalStateException` naming the issuer's notAfter / notBefore);
- otherwise notBefore = now, notAfter = min(requested end, issuer notAfter).

Every issuance path (`issueCertificate`, `issueIDevIDCertificate`, `signCSR`,
`issueCertificateWithProfile`) goes through `issueForPublicKey`, which calls
the function before anything is built or stored, so a refusal stores nothing.
`generateIntermediateCertificate` calls the end-date variant against the root.

## Slices, in order

1. This plan.
2. Refactor (behaviour unchanged): add `CertificateValidity.kt` with today's
   rule (now + days, no clamp, no refusal) and call it from both places.
3. Failing tests: `CertificateValidityTest` (fixed `Date` values, no clock):
   issuer with 10 years left gives now + days; 30 days left with a 365-day
   profile gives the issuer notAfter; expired issuer throws; issuer notBefore
   in the future throws; validityDays 0 and -1 throw; SubordinateCA default
   1825 days under an issuer with 2 years left is clamped.
4. Fix: the clamp and the refusals.
5. Docs: `docs/certificate-authority/engineering.md`, "Certificate validity".

## Out of scope

- The root's own validity, the CRL validity, `SSHCertificateAuthority`.
- `docs/certificate-authority/architecture.md` (held by an open PR).

## r2: issuance-level tests

Review: the tests above call `CertificateValidity` directly, so removing its
call from `AndroidCA` left the suite green. `AndroidCAIssuanceValidityTest`
drives `AndroidCA` itself on the JVM:

- seam: an internal `AndroidCA(context, openKeyStore)` constructor (the public
  one still opens the Android Keystore) and an internal
  `generateIntermediateCertificate`; the tests pass an in-memory KeyStore and
  store the CA certificates in mocked SharedPreferences;
- leaf under an issuer with 30 days left ends at the issuer's notAfter (and the
  persisted row says so); an issuer with years left keeps the profile days;
- intermediate under a root with 400 days left ends at the root's notAfter;
  an expired root signs no intermediate;
- expired issuer, not-yet-valid issuer, validityDays 0 and -1: no certificate,
  no persisted row, no issuance audit entry.

Each was checked to fail with the `CertificateValidity` call removed from the
matching `AndroidCA` path.
