# 352: TPM attestation parsing and quote binding

## Objective

TPM enrollment decides which desktop keys this phone's certificate authority
signs, and every byte it parses comes from the desktop. This row gives the
parsers direct JVM tests (`TpmPublicArea`, the quote and certify verifiers in
`TpmAttest.kt`, `TcgCsrIDevID`) and binds the PCR values the desktop reports to
the quote it signed.

## Slices, in order

1. `TpmPublicAreaTest`: RSA exponent 0 and explicit exponents, keyBits that
   disagree with the modulus, empty modulus, a 2B size past the end; ECC P-256,
   P-384 and P-521; unsupported curve and object type; a non-NULL symmetric
   definition and ECC KDF read past; `name()` under SHA-1, SHA-256 and SHA-384
   and an unsupported nameAlg; every prefix of a valid area. Records trailing
   bytes, an off-curve point, zero-length coordinates and RSA-1024.
2. `TpmQuoteBindingTest` and `TpmTestStructures` helpers (selection builder,
   pcrDigest, RSA-PSS and P-384 keys): RSA-PSS and P-384 quotes verify; PSS
   with maximum salt recorded; selection count 17 and a bitmap past the end
   refused. Binding: a matching digest verifies; a changed PCR value, a
   different bank and a missing selected index are refused.
3. `TcgCsrIDevIDBoundsTest`: every prefix refused as `TcgCsrException`;
   trailing bytes after the signature recorded; unreadable IAK and IDevID
   areas refused; `TpmCertifyVerifier` with an unrestricted attester recorded.
4. Bugs: a failing-test commit, then the fix.

## Out of scope

The wire format; the PCR policy (which indices are requested); minimum key
sizes and trailing-byte policy (reported below, not changed).
